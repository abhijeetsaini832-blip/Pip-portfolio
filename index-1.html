About Pip

What I am

A baby with a round skull face, glowing eyes and a crooked smile. Cute, never scary!

Curious, sleepy, a little clumsy and easily delighted.

Pip's Story

Scroll down and follow the dream route.

1. A yawn became a star

One sleepy night, a giant yawn sparkled into a star. It landed in the Midnight Nursery and turned into a baby with a skull-shaped smile. That was Pip!

2. The Midnight Nursery

The neighbourhood's little ghosts and creatures can't sleep without a lullaby. Pip got a tiny desk, a nightcap and a very important job.

3. Junior Dream Engineer

Pip builds lullabies, nightlights and bedtime dreams. Sometimes a gear falls off. Sometimes a dream comes out upside down. Everyone loves it anyway.

4. Tonight's route

Every night Pip floats over the rooftops delivering fresh dreams. Next stop: Senior Dream Engineer. But first, a nap.
